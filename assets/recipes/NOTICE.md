# FreshKeep local recipe library

The bilingual recipe instructions in recipes.json and the procedural vector
illustrations in lib/features/recipes/presentation/recipe_art.dart were written
and drawn for this FreshKeep project. No third-party recipe text or food
photographs were copied into this library. The illustrations are symbolic dish
drawings, not photographs of finished recipes.

Recipe times are estimates for two servings. Water and other staples are listed
explicitly; inventory matching does not assume the user owns them. Users review
ingredient identity, quantity, condition and suitability before cooking.

The bundled font has its separate SIL Open Font License in assets/fonts/OFL.txt.
Open Food Facts data and images used by the scanning feature retain their own
licenses; they are not the source of this recipe library.
